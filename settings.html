Last updated: May 2024

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us when you create an account, use our services, or communicate with us. This includes your name, email address, and any prompt content you create.

We use the information we collect to operate, maintain, and improve our services, process transactions, send notifications, and respond to your inquiries.

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share your information with service providers who assist us in operating our platform.
